# Era 9 of 9 — Local Control, Segmentation, and Automation

**Home Assistant · IoT · Matter · Z-Wave · Security**

## The current phase
The current focus is improving how the environment is segmented, managed, and automated. I am working to separate trusted clients, servers, management systems, and IoT devices while keeping the communication they need.

I am also moving more smart-home functionality toward Home Assistant and locally controlled technologies to reduce unnecessary cloud dependencies.

## What changed
Home Assistant now brings several parts of the lab into one operational workflow. A custom dashboard shows server status and uptime, supports controlled restart and update actions, and links to services such as Proxmox, AdGuard Home, Beszel, and external health monitoring.

## What I am exploring
**IoT segmentation · Matter · Z-Wave · Cross-VLAN services · Least-privilege access · Local-first design · Automation · Monitoring · Recovery**

## The journey so far
A repurposed laptop running Pi-hole grew into a platform for networking, Linux, self-hosting, virtualization, monitoring, and smart-home infrastructure. Each stage began with a problem to solve, something to learn, or a limit in the previous design.
