# Era 1 of 9 — Taking Control of DNS

**Pi-hole · Linux · DNS · Repurposed hardware**

## The starting point
I wanted network-wide ad filtering that I could run and understand myself. I connected an unmanaged Ethernet switch and a repurposed laptop running Pi-hole.

The laptop had been a Windows 10 system. I converted it to Linux and gave it one focused job: provide DNS filtering for the home network.

## What changed
- DNS filtering moved from individual devices to a service on the network.
- A spare laptop became always-on infrastructure.
- I began maintaining a Linux host and a service other devices depended on.

## What I learned
**Linux basics · DNS · Network services · Hardware reuse · Service administration**

## Why the lab grew
Running Pi-hole raised new questions about the network around it. I wanted to learn how managed switches worked and how to administer them.
