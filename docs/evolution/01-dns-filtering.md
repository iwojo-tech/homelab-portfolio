# Era 1 of 9 — Taking Control of DNS

**Pi-hole · Linux · DNS filtering · Repurposed hardware**

## The starting point
I wanted network-wide ad filtering, especially on my children's devices, that I could run and understand myself. I connected an unmanaged Ethernet switch and repurposed a Windows 10 laptop as a Linux host running Pi-hole.

DNS itself was not a new concept to me. I already worked with enterprise networking and DNS professionally. What was different was taking responsibility for deploying and maintaining a Linux-based DNS filtering service at home.

## What changed
- DNS filtering moved from individual devices to a shared service on the home network.
- A spare laptop became always-on infrastructure.
- I gained more hands-on practice with Linux administration and maintaining a service other devices relied on.

## Skills Applied & Developed
- **Applied:** DNS fundamentals, IP networking, and network troubleshooting from my professional background.
- **Developed:** Linux host administration, Pi-hole deployment, DNS filtering configuration, and ongoing service maintenance.
